About the game


After years of trying to move on from the loss of your former boyfriend, you believed that chapter of your life had finally come to an end. Then, on a sleepless night, an unexpected figure appears before you... someone claiming to be the very person you once loved.




General information


Genre: Visual Novel / Psychological Horror | Style: Choice-based narrative | Development: MiyaSayMiau | Platforms: PC, Mac, Linux, Android, and Web

Development


The game is still under development, with its story and world continuing to expand as new content is created. About the developer


Miya is a Brazilian independent developer known online as MiyaSayMiau. She uses she/her pronouns and describes herself as someone who is still learning and honing her skills as a developer. She began learning programming on her own, developing her own projects and learning along the way. In her posts, she also shows a strong interest in art, illustration, writing, and character creation, while also discussing her learning process and the challenges she faces when drawing. Miya maintains a very close relationship with the people who follow her work. She frequently answers questions, chats with fans, and shares updates, drawings, and happenings from her daily life as a creator. Her communication style is casual, humorous, and highly personal, fostering a community around her projects. Despite having an active online presence, information such as their age, legal name, city of residence, and date of birth is not reliably disclosed to the public.
